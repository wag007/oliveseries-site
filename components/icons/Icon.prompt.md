One-line: the brand's only two icons — a hairline arrow-up-right (every outbound link) and the close X (overlay dismiss); both are unfilled 1px strokes in a square hit area.

```jsx
<Icon name="arrow-up-right" size={48} color="var(--olive-white)" />
<Icon name="close" size={200} strokeWidth={2} />
```

Sizes seen in the source: 48px inside tiles, 157px in the newsletter bar, 200px for the overlay close.
